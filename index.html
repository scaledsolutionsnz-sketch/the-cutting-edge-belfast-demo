About Us

Your Local
Belfast Salon

The Cutting Edge Belfast is a welcoming, modern salon in the heart of Belfast, Christchurch. Rochelle and her talented team bring passion, precision, and personality to every appointment.

We stay current with the latest trends and techniques, from lived-in colour and balayage to colour corrections and keratin treatments, while always putting the health of your hair first.

Friendly & Relaxed Atmosphere

Walk in feeling stressed, leave feeling amazing. That's our promise to every client.

Colour Specialists

We take pride in our colour work, blondes, brunettes, reds, and everything in between.
