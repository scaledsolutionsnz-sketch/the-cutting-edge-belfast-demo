Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

The Cutting Edge Belfast ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and email when you submit a booking enquiry
  • Feedback submitted through our private feedback form
  • Basic website analytics such as page views and device type

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To book and confirm your salon appointments
  • To respond to your enquiries and messages
  • To improve our services using client feedback
  • We never sell your information to third parties

3. Data Storage

Enquiries are processed via StaticForms. Your data is stored securely and only accessible to The Cutting Edge Belfast team.

4. Your Rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information held by us.

5. Cookies

This website may use essential cookies for basic site functionality. No advertising or behavioural tracking cookies are used.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, email us at thecuttingedgebelfast@gmail.com or call 027 255 5880.
